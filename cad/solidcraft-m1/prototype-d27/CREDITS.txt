Mac mini M1 · Enclosure Lab · FDM D2.7 · Univera LLC

D2.7 replaces the Mac board locating collars with flat seating rings and simplifies the two-pin carrier for CMTPD and Mini-560. The D2.6 rear panel, front blank and side guide sample are unchanged. The kit is for unpowered fitting; physical assembly, powered use, cooling, button operation and wireless performance remain unverified. Print only the supplied STLs. The STEP and SolidCraft files are editable CAD, and the board GLB is a spatial reference.

Logic board, heatsink and antenna reference source: Quinn Nelson / SnazzyLabs, “Shrink the M1 Mac mini!”, Printables 139893, https://www.printables.com/model/139893-shrink-the-m1-mac-mini . Connector dimensions and plug examples: USB Implementers Forum, HDMI Licensing, IEC/FCC, and drawings from Same Sky, Lumberg, PIC Wire & Cable and VPI. Power modules: Radio Construct / Rozetka, CMTPD 483069554 and Mini-560 398197737. The plastic carrier works with modeled board and solder envelopes; verify the actual modules.
